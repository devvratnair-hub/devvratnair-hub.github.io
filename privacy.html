Privacy notice

Updated October 3, 2026.

Dragonfruit Technologies is the independent sole-proprietor consulting trade name of Devvrat Nair. This notice describes the website's services and privacy choices.

Project enquiries

Information you provide by email, booking, or chat is used to respond to your enquiry and arrange consulting conversations. This may include your name, contact details, organization, and project requirements. Keep initial enquiries at project level rather than sharing employee records, payroll data, or credentials.

Optional chat and analytics

HubSpot provides the chat interface and visitor analytics. Its scripts are blocked until you select Allow chat & analytics. Once enabled, HubSpot may process page visits, browser and device information, cookie identifiers, and information you submit through chat. Chat has an additional notice before you send a message.

Choosing Essential only keeps HubSpot scripts blocked. The website's content, email, phone, and Calendly links remain available. You can change your choice through Privacy settings in the footer. Withdrawing permission removes the widget, clears known HubSpot cookies set on this website, and reloads the page to stop its scripts. This does not delete information already submitted to HubSpot.

Storage and service providers

  • Privacy preference: this website stores your choice in your browser's local storage. It applies to this browser and website; clearing site data resets it.
  • HubSpot: when allowed, analytics and chat can use cookies such as hubspotutk, __hstc, and messagesUtk. See HubSpot's privacy policy.
  • Calendly: the booking link opens Calendly's website, where its own privacy notice and cookie controls apply. See Calendly's privacy notice.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this website and receives technical information needed to serve it. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts: your browser requests fonts from Google to display the site's typography. See Google Fonts privacy information.
  • External links: LinkedIn and other linked services follow their own policies when you visit them.

Questions or requests

For questions about information shared with Dragonfruit Technologies, or to request access, correction, or deletion, email devvrat.nair@hotmail.com. Browser preferences can be changed here; information already shared with a service provider may require a separate request.
